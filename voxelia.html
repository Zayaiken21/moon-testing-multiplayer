How to play

Nothing in Voxelia can be hurt. Animals run, hide, or come closer — that is the whole range of what happens between you and them.

W A S DWalk. Hold Shift to sprint. SpaceJump. Tap again mid-air once a Bramblehop follows you. MouseMove to look. Left button places, right button mines, middle button copies a block. Swap them in Settings. If the page blocks pointer capture, hold a button and drag to look. 1 – 9Choose a hotbar slot. The scroll wheel works too. EFeed, tame, collect from, or dismiss the nearest animal. Also gets you in and out of vehicles. FToggle flight in creative mode. LSubmarine lamps, while you are aboard. HHead lamp, once you are wearing dive goggles. GStar chart: every world the telescope has found. From orbit it is also your autopilot. LLand, when a world is close enough. MOpen the explored map. The little round map is clickable too. TChat with everyone in the room. VMicrophone on and off. QDrop what you are holding. Hold shift to drop the lot. BOpen your bag: satchel, crafting, furnace, and in creative every block there is. EscPause. GamepadLeft stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps, X interacts, triggers mine and place, d-pad changes slot. TouchLeft half of the screen is a movement stick, right half looks. Buttons sit along the right edge.

Getting off the ground

Build an engineer's bench, then a crew capsule, thrusters, fuel cells, a heat shield and a nav computer. Those make a Rocket — four seats, or eight in a Starliner. Set it down, climb in, hold the throttle and keep climbing: past the top of the sky you break into orbit. Up there the star chart picks your heading, a safety tether lets you step out and gather floating minerals, and Land brings you down on whatever world you are near. Every planet has its own sky, gravity and creatures.

Taming, step by step

Your hotbar starts empty. Mine a block and it drops on the ground for you to walk over — that is how the bar fills. Break the plants, leaves and coral in an area until you gather its food — Sunberries from Vale canopy, Tidefruit from coral, Lumenspores from glowing caps. Walk up to that biome's animal with the food in your satchel and press feed. It follows you from then on, lends you its ability, and leaves something to collect every so often.

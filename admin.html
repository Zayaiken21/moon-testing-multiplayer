What the game pays

Every figure here is in cents. Changes take effect on the very next animal anybody tames — there is no deploy and no restart. Anything that cannot work is corrected as you save it, and you are told what was corrected and why.

What each animal is worth

Common is zero on purpose: most of the world is common, so an ordinary animal is company rather than wages.

How much may be earned in a day

A daily limit below the most valuable animal is a limit that refuses that animal every time, so it is raised to meet it. Reaching the limit tops a player up to it exactly rather than turning them away: four cents of room and a thirty cent legendary pays four.

Membership codes
